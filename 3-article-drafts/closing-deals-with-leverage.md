# The Leverage Framework for Closing Deals

*How I learned to stop chasing perfect pitches and start building real leverage*

---

I used to think closing deals meant having the best product and smoothest pitch.

I was wrong.

After getting rejected 47 times in three months, I studied Taylor Sheridan's path from sleeping in a tent to owning a $500M empire. One insight changed everything.

**You don't need money to close deals. You need leverage.**

## The Three-Step Framework That Actually Works

**Step 1: Create undeniable value**
Sheridan got rejected by 100+ production companies with the same script. The 101st said yes. Why? He didn't change the script—he changed who he approached and how he positioned the value.

Your prospects don't care about features. They care about transformation.

**Step 2: Use wins to access bigger opportunities**
One successful client becomes social proof for ten bigger ones. Sheridan leveraged Yellowstone's success into multiple spinoffs. Each deal opened doors to the next.

Every closed deal is actually three deals: the current one, the referrals, and the credibility.

**Step 3: Structure for multiplication**
Sheridan doesn't just own a ranch—he charges $50,000/week for filming rights, $214,000 for training camps, $25 per cattle appearance.

One asset, multiple revenue streams.

## What Changed For Me

I stopped perfecting pitches and started building leverage. Instead of competing on price, I created value they couldn't get elsewhere. Instead of one-time deals, I structured ongoing partnerships.

Last quarter: closed three deals worth 2x my previous best year.

**The truth:** Persistence without leverage is just expensive. Leverage without persistence is wasted opportunity.

Both together? That's how you stop chasing deals and start closing them.

---

*Start with value. Build leverage. Structure for multiplication. Everything else is noise.*
