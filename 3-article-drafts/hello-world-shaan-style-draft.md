# I Want to Be Immortal (And You Should Too)

Finally!

I'm here writing my memoirs.
**Not because I'm dying.**

Because I want to **never die**.
## The Immortality Hack

You can't live forever.

But you **can** create forever.

I want my kids to find this someday and think: "Oh. *This* is who dad really was."

**That's immortality right there.**

## The Prison of Perfect

For *years* I wanted to build my digital real estate.

But I kept making excuses:
- No time ⏰
- No tech skills 💻  
- Tools too complex 🤷‍♂️

**Every excuse was just fear wearing a costume.**

Fear of starting something imperfect.

Fear of actually doing something that mattered.

## The Truth That Slapped Me

**I should have just started something.**

*Anything.*

Perfect is the enemy of done. And done is the enemy of never started.

Because waiting for the perfect moment is just another way of saying "never."  

And "never" is the opposite of immortal.

**The path of least resistance isn't lazy—it's smart.**

Build slowly, brick by brick.

## Your Immortality Project

Here's my question:

What's your immortality project?

**What's the thing your kids will find someday and think: "This is who they really were."**

The uncomfortable truth?

**You're already writing your legacy.**

Every day you don't start is another page in "what could have been."

Every day you *do* something is another page in "what actually happened."

Which story do you want to tell?

## Build.Fun.Free.

That's it!!! That's the whole philosophy.

Build something that matters to you.

Have fun doing it.  

Free yourself from the prison of perfection.

Stop waiting -> Start building.

Your future self will thank you.

*Let's go 💪*
