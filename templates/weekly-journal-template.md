# Weekly Journal Template

Use this structure for consistent weekly insight generation.

## Template Structure

```markdown
# Weekly Insights: [Date Range] - [Theme Title]

## This Week's Journey

[Opening paragraph that sets context and preview main themes - 100-150 words]

## Key Insights & Developments

### [Theme 1: Primary Challenge/Development]
[Detailed exploration of main theme with supporting details - 200-300 words]

**Connected Insights:**
- [Related insight from connected content]
- [Another related insight or solution approach]

### [Theme 2: Secondary Development/Solution]
[Second major theme or development - 200-300 words]

**Supporting Perspectives:**
- [World-view reference or external validation]
- [Connected insight that supports or extends this theme]

### [Theme 3: Learning/Win (if applicable)]
[Positive development, breakthrough, or learning - 150-200 words]

## Patterns & Connections

[Meta-insights about how this week's themes connect to broader patterns - 150-200 words]

## Looking Forward

[Forward-looking perspective on implications and next areas of focus - 100-150 words]

---
*Total target: 1000-1500 words*
```

## Style Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- **Conversational but thoughtful** - like sharing insights with a peer
- **Authentic and vulnerable** - honest about struggles and uncertainties  
- **Growth-oriented** - focus on learning and progress
- **Specific over generic** - concrete examples and details

### Structure Patterns  
- **Story-driven** - use narrative arc and progression
- **Example-rich** - include specific situations and outcomes
- **Connected thinking** - show relationships between ideas
- **Forward-looking** - end sections with implications or next steps

### Quality Markers
- **Insight density** - every paragraph should offer value
- **Specificity** - avoid vague business-speak
- **Personal perspective** - your unique viewpoint and experience  
- **Actionable themes** - ideas readers can apply or learn from

## Content Integration

### From Raw Ideas
- Use recent struggles, wins, metrics, observations as primary material
- Follow narrative threads across multiple entries
- Transform daily observations into weekly themes

### From Connected Insights
- Leverage wikilinked content for supporting evidence
- Use older insights to provide context and continuity
- Show evolution of thinking over time

### From Training Data
- Reference world-view content for external validation
- Use great-writing patterns for structural inspiration
- Maintain quality benchmarks from style profile

## Template Variables

When using this template, customize:
- **[Date Range]**: Specific week being covered
- **[Theme Title]**: Compelling title that captures the week's core narrative
- **[Theme 1/2/3]**: Specific themes drawn from your recent activity
- **Connected Insights**: Pulled from wikilinked content and YAML metadata
- **Supporting Perspectives**: World-view references and external wisdom

This template provides structure while maintaining flexibility for authentic storytelling and insight development.
