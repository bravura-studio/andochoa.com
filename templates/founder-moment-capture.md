## Content Development Path 🛤️

1. **Moment Captured** → Raw emotion/facts recorded
2. **Reflection Added** → What does this mean?
3. **Lesson Extracted** → What can others learn?
4. **Story Structured** → Using SCAR framework
5. **Value Added** → Templates/resources to share
6. **Community Engaged** → Posted with discussion prompts

## Publishing Decision Framework ✅

**Always Share:**
- Learning moments with clear lessons
- Vulnerable moments with growth attached
- Community-helping content
- Authentic struggles with hope

**Maybe Share:**
- Personal family impact (consider privacy)
- Specific revenue numbers (context dependent)
- Team/partner conflicts (protect relationships)
- Technical details (audience dependent)

**Never Share:**
- Customer private information
- Legal/compliance issues
- Pure negativity without growth
- Competitors' private information

---

*The best build-in-public content comes from real moments, captured immediately, shared authentically.*
