## Weekly Founder Journal

**Format:** Raw, reflective essays (200-300 words).  
**Key Sections to Always Include:**

- **🔥 This Week’s Fire:** (1-2 sentences) What urgent problem dominated?  
    *Example: “Fixing payment failures costing $1.2K/day”*

- **📈 Metric Spotlight:** Share 1 key KPI with context (good/bad).  
    _Example: “Churn spiked to 8.4% → tracked to broken onboarding Step 3”_

- **🤯 Mental Shift:** Your biggest mindset change.  
    _Example: “Stopped obsessing over competitors after talking to 10 users”_

- **🚧 What’s Broken:** Explicitly state unresolved struggles.  
    _Example: “Our docs suck – anyone know a technical writer?”_

- **🎁 Ask/Offer:** Give value or request help.  
    _Example: “Free template: Our churn diagnosis checklist”_
