\[you walk in and see me sitting cross-legged, lighting candles, eyes closed\]

*Welcome my friend…I’m Shaan the Seer™ – The Oracle of the East Bay. The Prince of Predictions.*

**You:** Oh.. I was actually just looking for a bathroom. But I guess I do have a question for you…

*Speak it.*

**You:** Well, I’m pretty worried about AI. It seems great - but I’m worried about the future. Is this going to replace my job?

*Mmmm… I see… a letter.*

**You:** A letter?

*Silence! Your words are disturbing my vision… the letter…X? No… C? no…AH YES. There it is. The letter is clear to my inner eye now. I see the letter K.*

**You:** The letter K..?

*Yes. The future is shaped like a K. There are two paths.*

*The first path, looks like this…\*\*takes out glitter pen* \*\*

![](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXekMeCmfVx0eEoEkEGvPReEvd1LO-P6VEVqRsWsYYd1JtaVYbGYjAnfu0bQ6wIXROQhw0y3dpy1nz0epwTENGxL8Uh6gxE8xWy2_eJNtJTJsnyvfUu64ghq1nYK82TpbVnIz5G9hg?key=Yxcbl3dJ2A99kTrqym1TTwaX)

**You:** Oh sh\*t! 80% of my skills will be replaced by AI?

*Yes dear. You are a horse. And Henry Ford just put a car on the road. The outlook is grim.*

**You:** C’mon don’t you think that’s a little dramatic?

*Are you questioning Shaan the Seer?*

**You:** No no, of course not. But wait, you said the future is shaped like a K.. what’s the upside?

![](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcRHHBpfPMNcsvLlgcHjWbOC_Hc7YLb8slQiNr5BwzJ0qaLjkRc1JiDo-Y2wS-MA487rYXNp-d_tSpyTDe9AKN_5rUymv0PjREXw2GQxUZmWWA6G9DsEECwa631qHPCterdHi5C?key=Yxcbl3dJ2A99kTrqym1TTwaX)

*You have a choice. Either get replaced by AI, or learn to use it and become 10X more productive.*

**You**: Got it. So how *exactly* do I do this?

*\*\*turns down volume knob of “mystic ASMR summer jams2024”* \*\*  
  
*Listen kid, here’s what you need to do:*

Stop thinking of your job as 1 big thing. Instead, think of your job as a “bundle of tasks”.

For example, if you’re a sales rep – your job is a bundle of tasks:

- Prospecting potential customers
- Drafting cold emails
- Tracking response rates
- Scheduling calls
- Doing the calls
- Writing notes during the call
- Followup emails
- Drafting proposals
- Updating your CRM
- Etc..

How many of those tasks can AI do today? Maybe only 2-3 out of 20 can be significantly boosted with AI today.

But AI is improving fast. Soon, half your tasks will be handled by AI.

Write this down - **in the future being able to “manage robots” will be more valuable than managing humans.**

So learn to use AI tools. Make them your baby-back-b\*tch.

**You:** Make them my baby back wha–?

*You heard me. Now get out of here, I have customers waiting.*

**You:** Thanks, Shaan the Seer. I’m going to go do this!

*WAIT!*

**You:** What? Are you having another vision

*Yes… I see … numbers…. 15%....20%...25%....*

**You:** Noooooooooooo!!!
