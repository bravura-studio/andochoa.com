This is the **One Minute Blog (OMB)**. An email of good stuff for you to think about over the weekend, that takes about a minute to read.

*Today’s word count: 190*

*It took me* ***49s*** *to read this.*

Sorry guys, I’m too busy today - I can’t write my usual **One Minute Blog** post.

Or can I?

Maybe it’s not all-or-nothing.

Maybe I could write something simple. Something small.

Maybe something is better than nothing.

Am I letting perfection be the enemy of progress?

My piano teacher asked me for business advice, I told her she should start a youtube channel.

What if she took the thing she’s insanely good at (playing piano), but instead of just teaching 1 person at a time, teach millions of people every month on youtube.

*“That’s a great idea. I’m going to do that.”*

That was 4 weeks ago….still no channel.

The idea costs $0. It would take her 30 minutes to start her channel. Maybe another hour to make a video.

What’s the holdup?

She’s overthinking it. (Just like I almost over-thought today’s blog)

Our desire to do it well stops us from doing it at all.

Nike was right. *“Just do it”.*

The million dollar word there is *“just”*. As in, *“just freakin do it already”.*

Stop thinking about it. Stop talking about it.

Progress over Perfection.

Let it rip.

\-Uncle Shaan
