90% of companies fail to execute their strategy.

Here is how the top 10% actually deliver:

Let me introduce a powerful tool:

The Strategy Pyramid.

Originally shared by Wendy McGuiness (2011):

It breaks strategy down into 9 essential pillars, grouped into 3 key levels:

Purpose, Strategy, Execution.

A) Purpose

1) Mission

Defines why the company exists.

It’s your reason for being.

2) Values

Your guiding principles:

How decisions are made and what you stand for.

3) Vision

Where you’re going.

The future you’re building.

B) Strategy

4) Strategic Intent

Big-picture goals and how you plan to reach them.

5) Drivers

The levers that will move your business:

Growth, innovation, efficiency, etc.

6) Enablers

The resources and capabilities:

Tech, people, capital - that make strategy happen.

C) Execution

7) Targets & Initiatives

Clear actions and measurable goals.

8) Performance Indicators

Track what matters.

KPIs that show real progress.

9) Strategy Map

Make the strategy visible.

Ensure everyone’s aligned.

Together, these 9 components help you:

i. Define your purpose

ii. Build a real strategy

iii. Execute it successfully

All three levels matter.

Miss one, and the whole thing breaks.
