Product design Eisenhower Matrix

🔷 High clarity, low risk: measure, iterate, optimize

🔷 High clarity, high risk: absolutely nail the fundamentals

🔷 Low clarity, high risk: gather info and proceed carefully

🔷 Low clarity, low risk: execute as fast and cheaply as possible
