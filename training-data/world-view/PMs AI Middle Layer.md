**Carl Vellotti** @carlvellotti [2025-09-09](https://x.com/carlvellotti/status/1965469978943168952)

Engineers: "It takes years to build real software, stop trying."

Grifters: "Build anything in 30 seconds with AI!"

Truth: There's a middle layer where PMs absolutely dominate.

Going from a helpless vibe coder well to doing real AI-assisted development is based on these things:

🏗️ Understanding architecture → Knowing how pieces connect

🔄 Using version control → Saving your work properly

🎮 Manual testing → Clicking through everything like a user would

💭 Documenting decisions → Writing down "why"

🗺️ Planning ahead → Breaking down work ahead of time

These aren't "engineering" skills.

They're actually closer to intuitive PM skills!

All you need to do put in the reps to make them second nature.

And you learn them by doing.

Not by studying.

Not by getting a CS degree.

Just by building one small thing.

Then another.

Then another.

Until suddenly you realize... you're shipping so truly complex software.

And as models improve, this line will continue to get flatter, letting you do more and more with the skills you already have.

The Full Stack PM revolution is just getting started.
