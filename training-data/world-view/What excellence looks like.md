**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308456793477136)

When interviewing for a new role, grill the hiring manager on what excellence looks like.

If they can’t paint a vivid picture of what it takes to win, run as fast as you can.

Here are some examples:

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308459620553103)

"What is this team's North Star metric?"

For Airbnb, this is "Nights Booked."

It's an easy to understand number that lets everyone ask, "Will this work I'm doing help or hurt?"

It's remarkably powerful for building autonomy. And incredibly hard to navigate without one.

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308462325825889)

"What is the most important problem to solve right now?"

\- Is it immediate and tangible?

\- Or distant & poorly defined?

\- Or unrealistic laundry list?

Business physics tells us there's always a single bottleneck. Impactful leaders pick the right problems.

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308465022795861)

"What do I bring to the team that is currently missing?"

You are being added to a system. It might be an implicit system or an explicit one. But it's there.

If you're not bringing something different to the team, it makes it 10x harder to stand out.

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308467686080999)

How do your top performers stand out?"

You don't want a leader who can spot excellence. You want one who can define it, who can appreciate different versions of it.

Ask for examples and listen carefully. Chances are the examples will reveal a different reality.

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308470282375294)

"Who is world class at what we do?"

For 99% of jobs, someone has done it before.

Does the leader you're talking to know who they are or pretend they don't exist?

If they're not a) studying what works and b) actively building atop or against that, they're playing on hard mode.

---

**Dave Kline** @dklineii [2025-06-29](https://x.com/dklineii/status/1939308473180709305)

In business, winning creates opportunity. And for you, opportunity offers growth.

Unfortunately, a leader who can’t clearly define winning has already lost.

Choose games you're uniquely suited to win.
