→ Hero Section

Keep the most important things at the top.

Add an interactive demo.

Use punchy copywriting.

→ Problem agitation + transformation

Use vivid “before after” storytelling.

Clearly mention the pain points.

Explain how your product will solve it.

Incorporate numbers

→ Pricing

Use decoy effect & show three tiers

Center the premium plan visually

Label the premium as “most popular”

![The image displays a pricing page for a photography software subscription service, featuring three tiers: 'Starter', 'Pro', and 'Premium'.
