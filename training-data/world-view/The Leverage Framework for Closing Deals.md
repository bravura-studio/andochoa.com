3\. Make Assets Pay For Themselves

Taylor turned the ranch into a profit center.

Paramount now pays him:

\- $50,000 per week just to film on the property

\- $214,000 for a cowboy camp to train actors

\- And even charges $25 a head for cattle to appear on screen

Genius.

This proves you don’t need a gazillion dollars, a ton connections, or “luck” to be successful.

You need to follow 3 conceptually simple steps:

\- Step 1: Build something valuable enough that someone will pay attention

\- Step 2: Use that credibility to access bigger opportunities and partnerships

\- Step 3: Structure those new assets to generate new streams revenue

The best part? Every step de-risks the next one.
