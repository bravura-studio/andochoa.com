As founders, our main job is navigating the five distinct phases of chaos...

Each one has a different goal. Different playbook.

And I see alot of founders get stuck because they’re using the wrong playbook for the stage they’re in.

Here are the 5 phases every entrepreneur should know about, and how to break through:

Building a company is damn hard.

The difference between huge success and building a zombie.

So, don't become a zombie.

Ready? Here we go...

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284147674591581)

1️⃣ Pre-Product Phase (Est. Size: $0 revenue)

→ Find your co-conspirators.

→ Test your ideas through research, convos, surveys, etc.

→ Then start building.

#I Goal: Launch product and get in front of users ASAP.

---

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284149163548702)

2️⃣ Product Market Fit (Est. Size: $I - $IM revenue)

Find your niche and tweak features.

Be prepared to evolve. Your first idea is rarely the one that sticks.

#I Goal: Product-market-fit with customers who pay and stay.

---

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284150715416666)

3️⃣ Initial Scale (Est. Size: $I-IOM revenue)

Create a reliable system to acquire customers profitably.

Don't spread yourself too thin during this phase - very common!

#I Goal: Dominate one major distribution channel.

---

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284152120566102)

4️⃣ Scale (Est. Size: SIO-I00M rev.)

Now you need to:

• Layer on more distribution

• Release new products

• Strengthen upgrade/sell path

#I Goal: Create distribution synergies through multi-channel and/or multi-product.

---

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284153429172621)

5️⃣ IPO-Ready (Est. Size: $I00M - $500M)

You might not go public.

But now you've got the swagger of a company that could.

This phase is all about fine-tuning unit economics.

You're almost there.

---

**Scot Chisholm** @scotchisholm [2025-06-18](https://x.com/scotchisholm/status/1935284155417338338)

An exit could happen at any phase...

You could go public or sell the company.

Or you could partially sell, and keep skin in the game.

Goal = create options for yourself as you grow!
