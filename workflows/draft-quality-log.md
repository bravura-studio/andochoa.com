# Draft Quality Assessment

*This file tracks the quality and performance of Draftsmith Agent generated drafts*

## Quality Assessment Framework

### Content Quality (1-10)
- **Insight Density**: Novel or valuable ideas per paragraph
- **Specificity**: Concrete examples vs. vague observations  
- **Narrative Coherence**: Logical flow and story progression
- **Theme Integration**: How well themes connect and support each other

### Voice Consistency (1-10)
- **Style Profile Match**: Adherence to learned voice patterns
- **Authenticity**: Feels genuinely like your writing
- **Tone Consistency**: Maintains appropriate tone throughout
- **Personality Markers**: Distinctive voice characteristics present

### Structural Quality (1-10)
- **Template Adherence**: Follows weekly journal structure appropriately
- **Section Balance**: Good distribution of content across sections
- **Transition Quality**: Smooth connections between themes and sections
- **Opening/Closing Strength**: Compelling beginning and ending

### Training Data Integration (1-10)
- **World-View Relevance**: External references add genuine value
- **Great-Writing Patterns**: Uses effective structural techniques
- **Reference Balance**: Not too many/few external connections
- **Integration Naturalness**: Training data feels organic, not forced

### Overall Editing Required (1-10)
- **10**: Publish-ready, no editing needed
- **8-9**: Minor tweaks, <5 minutes editing
- **6-7**: Good foundation, 5-15 minutes editing  
- **4-5**: Substantial editing needed, 15-30 minutes
- **1-3**: Major revision required, >30 minutes
