For years I wanted to build my digital real estate.

Always postponed. Lack of time. Lack of skills. No-code tools too rigid. The perfect setup never arrived.

---

The truth is embarrassing: I should have just started. Anything. The path of least resistance. One brick at a time.

Instead I spent years waiting for perfect conditions that don't exist.

---

So I'm starting now. Writing my memoirs on the world wide web.

Someday I want to look back and recall what I did. The missteps. The wins. The learnings.

I want my kids to read this.

---

The best time to start was yesterday. Now is the second best.

Build. Fun. Free.
