I spent years wanting to build something online. My own space. My own digital real estate.

Always postponed.

Wrong tools. Wrong time. Not enough skills. The excuses were endless and they all sounded reasonable.

The truth? I should have just started. Anything. The path of least resistance. One brick at a time.

I wanted to create something my kids could read someday. A record of what I did, what I learned, where I stumbled. Not a highlight reel — a real account.

So I finally started. No perfect setup. No grand plan. Just: start.

The best time was yesterday. Now is the second best.

If you've been waiting for the right moment to build your thing — it's not coming. The moment is when you decide to stop waiting.
