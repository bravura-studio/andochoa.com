## Posting Times

- **X**: 14:00-16:00 PT (catches US East morning + EU afternoon)
- **LinkedIn**: 08:00-09:00 PT (professionals checking feed at start of day)

## Daily Engagement Habit (every day, 10-15 min)

- Reply thoughtfully to 5-10 posts from builders in your space
- Hashtags to monitor: #buildinpublic, indie hackers, AI builders, solopreneurs
- Not generic "great post!" — add your perspective, share your experience
- Follow accounts 1-3 steps ahead of you
- This is how you get discovered before you have followers

## LinkedIn-Specific Rules

- Put the link to the full blog post in the FIRST COMMENT, not in the post body
- LinkedIn deprioritizes posts with external links in the body
- Add the comment immediately after posting

## Day 10 Review Checklist

After completing the first 10 days, answer these:

- [ ] Which posts got the most impressions?
- [ ] Which got the most engagement (replies, reposts, bookmarks)?
- [ ] X singles vs X threads — which format performed better?
- [ ] X vs LinkedIn — where is the audience more responsive?
- [ ] Which topic resonated most? (layoffs, animal spirit, freedom gap, alive)
- [ ] Did any replies/engagement lead to new connections or conversations?
- [ ] What time of day performed best?

Use answers to inform the next 10-day cycle. Double down on what worked. Drop what didn't.
