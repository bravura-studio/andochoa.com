A potential customer loved my product. Great questions. Genuine excitement. "I can see myself using this every day."

Then I mentioned the price and his voice changed.

A week later he signed with a competitor. Three times the cost. For a worse product.

That's not rejection. Rejection I can handle. That's polite interest — and it's the most dangerous thing a builder can hear, because it sounds like progress but it's actually silence.

I built a mantra for myself: Build. Fun. Free.

Build products. Have fun doing it. Enjoy the freedom.

Two out of three. I've got BUILD and FUN nailed. But FREEDOM? I'm fighting for the next day. Dipping into savings. Watching the runway shorten.

Maybe I'm talking to the wrong people. Maybe the pain isn't strong enough. Maybe they just don't trust a guy with no brand and a better product.

I don't know yet. But I know I can't go back.

Anyone else building something and stuck in the "polite interest" trap?
