A guy loved my product. Asked great questions. Said he'd use it every day.

I mentioned price. His voice changed.

A week later he signed with a bigger company for 3x the cost. For a worse product.

That's not rejection. That's polite interest. And polite interest is the most dangerous thing a builder can hear.

---

I have three products right now. All getting the same response: nods, smiles, "this is really interesting."

Zero pull. Nobody banging on the door. Nobody losing sleep without what I'm building.

They're curious, not desperate. And curious doesn't pay the bills.

---

My mantra: Build. Fun. Free.

I've got BUILD nailed — three products from scratch. FUN too — I don't mind the late nights, the boring tasks, the rejections. It's all mine.

But FREE? I'm fighting for the next day. Dipping into savings. Watching the runway shorten.

---

The worst part isn't the money. It's the thought that creeps in when the laptop closes.

What if none of this leads anywhere? What if I have to go back?

I don't know yet. But I know I can't go back.
