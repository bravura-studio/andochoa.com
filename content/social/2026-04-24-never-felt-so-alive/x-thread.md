There are two kinds of pain.

The first one drains you. You sit at a desk doing work that doesn't matter, for people who don't care, inside a machine that would replace you tomorrow.

You go home empty.

---

The second kind fills you up.

I skip lunch. Work through the night. Everything breaks at the same time. I dip into savings for the first time in my life.

And yet. I have never felt so alive.

---

1.5 years deep. I've thought about quitting more times than I can count.

Just get a regular job. Cruise through life. Collect the paycheck.

But the thought of going back haunts me more than the struggle of going forward.

---

Building is both my escape from the pain and my path through it.

Every day I'm getting better and faster. I can feel it compounding in ways I can't fully see yet.

If I didn't believe that, why would I have gone down this path?
