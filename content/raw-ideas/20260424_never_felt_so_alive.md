# Never Felt So Alive — Deep Dive

## Original content note

Despite all the challenges and feelings of despair - everything seems to work against you or fail at the same time, all the time - never felt so alive! At the end of the day, I have a feeling of joy and accomplishment. That I am moving forward to achieve my goals. And somehow all this actions will compound to something great. The honeymoon period is long gone. Already have passed 1.5 years. Many times I have thought about giving it all up - just get a regular job. Cruise through live. Collect the pay check and move on. However, just the thought of having a regular job again - it haunts me. Just a complete sense of despair. Like I have depression. I never want to stop working on my products. Every minute away I have the feeling I am wasting my time. Just want to come back to it as soon as possible.

## Deep Dive Conversation (2026-04-24)

**Q1: You're choosing one type of suffering over another. What makes the builder's suffering worth it? What's different about the pain?**

The builder's suffering makes me feel alive. I want to chase it. I want to keep going for hours. I skip lunch. I work through the night. It is fuel that lights my fire. It hurts, but makes me go forward. To push against it, because deep down I know that this will compound in ways that I can't think of right now, but will pay back in multiples.

The regular job pain is different. It takes the energy out of me. I feel I don't want to do it. That I will not have a fulfilling life. It just takes away and leaves me with an empty soul that is just moving along through life.

**Q2: What was the closest you actually came to quitting? A specific moment?**

My bank account balance. I always lived life without looking at the balance. I live frugally and always knew that day to day expenses were all taken care one way or the other. Then I was at a position where I needed to go to my savings account just to make ends meet. I think I will never shake this feeling off, but this is precisely what is fueling me right now.

Recently I took a service contract. Trading money for time. It was draining on my energy as well, but I was able to feel comfortable again. However, the tmaker partnership will be paused and I am worried right now, again. It is a rollercoaster. A bit lost right now. What is going to be next? Have some money on the street to collect, but that won't get me too far as well.

But again when I focus on building again. It fuels me. I feel the fire to continue and forget for a moment all the struggles that I am living through right now. I guess this helps me cope. However, it will not last long enough — my wife will make that uncomfortable question one day and it will trigger again angst. That I am not worthy. That I do nothing of value and can't sustain my family.

**Q3: Is building an escape FROM the pain, or the path THROUGH it?**

Both. It is a way to escape and a path through it because I have to believe that this short term pain will lead me to long term fulfillment and joy. That I will come out on top and happy. I am becoming better everyday and faster. If I did not believe it, why should I have gone down this path?

**Q4: What do you want your kids to feel when they read this ten years from now?**

Create your own belief system and when everything is falling apart around you and you are feeling lost. Look up to your north star and embrace your belief system. Follow that. Understand that today is not permanent, but a stepping stone in the right direction.
