The first question was: why am I doing this? Then came the despair. Why this does not work? Then it clicked back at me. I am making this bet to take advantage of the world cup window. However, there is a bigger underlying reason.

My kid likes to watch football highlights. He is my audience, right next to me when we sit together on the sofa. While he was watching his highlights, I was on the phone with Ferro building at the same time...thinking that would be funny if he was watching my channels without knowing.

That would be awesome to me. My kid wants to watch some football highlights and without knowing he is watching the channels I built. If he watches someone else's content why can't he watch mine? That is what I am aiming for. Evergreen content for my kid to watch about football.

I was already down into it. Building furiously while he was watching. I already made my mind in the spot. Let's get something up and running with enough quality that my kid won't notice the difference and starts watching my channels.

When we were stuck in a loop. The loops are the worst part. Me giving specific feedback about something wrong and then comes back exactly the same. But at least we were uncovering new bugs and not stuck on the same. However, it is very taxing not making progress. I already talked about this on other long form post we wrote recently.

**Q4: The bar you're setting is "my kid won't notice the difference." Has that bar changed what you build compared to if you were just optimizing for views?**

I know the quality bar — it is my kid. By watching his behaviour while watching the highlights.

When something is off for him in a video he immediately changes to another. It is primal. He might not be able to explain it, but he feels it. It is raw, brutal and no mercy. Like Julius Caesar deciding a gladiator fate by the mere fact that it is entertaining or not.

My reading of the situation: usually when the video stalls and footage is not pure football action. If it goes off script into story mode it will get cut.

**Q5: You're building this for him but he doesn't know. Is there a version of this where you tell him? Or is the whole point that he never knows — that the content earns its place on its own, without the "dad made this" card?**

Content earns its place on its own. That's when I will know that I made something great.

Maybe he will know in the future. Maybe when he reads my blog in a couple of years. I don't know. But the only card I want to play is the quality card from my kid's eyes. No swipe. Seeing the video through and through.
