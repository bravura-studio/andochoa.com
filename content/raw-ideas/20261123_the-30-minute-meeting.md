# Deep Dive: Cofounder not putting in the work

**Q1 (Ferro):** Who's the cofounder? What happened today that made this the topic — the moment, not the summary.

**A:** The human cofounder is for striva-app and striva-dental. We started the two year journey together and worked really close on several projects. She works hard, but a lot of times did not deliver on her word. She would set the target and then not deliver. I understand why — a lot of things happening at once that required her attention. But it was her that defined the target and deadline. When I did that I always delivered. Life happens, some times are harder than others, but I always made the effort to deliver on my word. She always said she was shooting for the stars and even if she under-delivered the progress was good. But more often than not the projects we were on together didn't move on her side, or not at the speed we expected. There might be some skill issues too — often she needed my help to solve problems that I picked up and solved somewhat easily. She just needed to put in the work and rounds. Most recently she took a job like me. Very demanding, barely any time for Striva — at least 4 months doing nothing. But what triggered today: last week she said she wanted to align and talk about the future. I gave her the choice of date and time and she postponed it 3 times. I was always available. On the last postpone she didn't even say she couldn't make it — I had to ask, then she replied she couldn't.

**Q2 (Ferro):** That's a priority signal, not a calendar problem. After two years of this pattern — do you still want her as cofounder, or are you holding on out of loyalty to the journey? What's the thing you haven't said out loud?

**A:** I am willing to give her a last chance: 3 strikes and she is out. That was going to be my proposal for the meeting. I would take striva-dental and she would be CEO of striva-app. striva-app will only be building the foundations and infrastructure for finance teams to work on top. No clients, just build. I will bring striva-app clients from striva-dental to use the infrastructure, at least in the beginning. But if even a 30 minute meeting is impossible, then the outcome is almost certain that she needs to go.

**Q3 (Ferro):** You already told me the ending. Strip her out of it — what has being the guy who always delivers actually cost you? Is being the reliable one lonely? What does this teach you about who you build with next?

**A:** It becomes lonely and frustrating when the other side does not deliver. It taught me that the other person needs to commit fully to the path or the exit needs to be swift. Also, exit conditions should be agreed from the beginning.
