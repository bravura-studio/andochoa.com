## Deep Dive Conversation

**Q: Walk me through the moment you decided this feature needed this level of engineering. What triggered the sprint?**

A: We are building striva. But it has a more general purpose - can be applied to every SMB in Portugal. However, we needed to niche down and focus on one market. That lead us to dental clinics. Two friends of mine from my knok days are now running a consulting company specialising in dental clinics - perfect match for us. They already have clients with measurable pain. They have the insights. They are in the trenches with the owners. They have their trust. That's when we come in. We started talking and we quickly realised that if we want to compete in the dental clinics space we need a full suite of features that are non negotiable. The clinics agenda is one of them. The owners will buy or kill our product by how good we are with the agenda. That's the bread and butter of their business and they are not willing to change providers if the agenda is not substantially better. The incumbents on this market already have a lot of features and already integrate what striva can offer regarding the finance with the agenda. Even if the software looks like from the 80s it is so entrenched in the market that it is hard to replace. However, we know by source - my friends - that they are willing to switch and add AI layers if we deliver top notch quality, UI/UX and workflows for their core business processes. And the main one is the agenda. But if it is not better, easier to manage and bullet proof, they rather stick with the old and clunky software that they already know how to operate.

**Q: That sprint on June 7th — 17 commits, the whole PRD-0034 redesign. What specifically was broken or confusing that made you go that deep in one day? Was it something your friends reported from the clinics, something you saw yourself, or something else?**

A: We put together a demo for my friends and they tested it for a whole week. By the end of the week they gave us their honest feedback. It was good, but we need to add improvements and have the simplest and bullet proof workflows for the agenda. That means covering all use cases and link that with the invoicing side. It has to be something that my friends are proud to show to their customers and put their name behind it. We started by using an open source project for dental clinics management as our foundation and now it is all about making it right, make it ours and add our craft. So we can say this is our take for this problems and this is how we are going to solve it. We are now starting to think about striva as the platform that we can build the verticals on top. Our first vertical is dental clinics management. One day, we will reach a point where others can build their own verticals on top of striva. For now, it will be us that will build those verticals to proof the point and get the market. Dental clinics need 3 things - agenda, people and invoicing. Do all this 3 right and you have the market. Then we can start layering the differentiators on top.

**Q: The 10 implementation units in PRD-0034 — was there a moment where you thought "this is too much for where we are"? Or did it always feel necessary?**

A: It always felt necessary. This will be the killer feature. The customers will buy or pass on our software solely based on how good the agenda is. The core workflows need to be there and bullet proof. All the use cases and edge cases should have a resolution and a follow up. Then we add our take and differentiators that solve problems that my friends already identified and are using custom workflows and excel sheets to solve because no other provider has the capabilities and are too slow implementing them or not even looking.

**Q: When your friends tested the demo for a week, did they know how it was built? And does it matter to them — or do they only care about what it feels like to use?**

A: They know how we are building it. They understand that now everyone needs to be AI pilled. But there are few that have the skills and willingness to do it. Dental clinics owners could probably do it themselves, but they don't have the time or the will. Therefore, they prefer to pay someone to do it for them. We all only care about the final outcome and if it solves a problem. Then we know it has value.
