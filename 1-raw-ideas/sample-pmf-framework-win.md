# Product-Market Fit Framework Win

Just had a breakthrough conversation with Sarah about how we should think about product-market fit. We've been measuring it all wrong.

The traditional approach is surveys and NPS scores - asking people "how would you feel if you couldn't use our product anymore?" But that's backward-looking and often misleading.

Sarah shared Superhuman's approach: they focus on leading indicators instead of lagging ones. The key insight is that PMF isn't a binary state - it's a spectrum you climb.

Their framework:
1. **Activation rate**: % of signups that complete the core workflow
2. **Retention cohorts**: Weekly active usage patterns
3. **Organic growth rate**: Word of mouth and referrals
4. **Value realization time**: How long until users see benefit

What clicked for me: we need to measure the *journey toward value*, not just satisfaction after value is delivered.

For us, this means tracking:
- Time from signup to first successful integration (currently 3.2 days)
- Weekly usage patterns in first 30 days
- Feature adoption sequence (which features predict retention?)
- Customer effort score for key workflows

The real revelation: PMF isn't about having happy customers. It's about having customers who can't imagine working without you because you've become essential to their daily workflow.

This changes everything about how we prioritize features. Instead of asking "what do customers want?" we should ask "what makes us irreplaceable in their workflow?"
