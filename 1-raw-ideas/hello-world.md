# Hello World

Finally! Welcome. 

I am here just writing my memoirs. Someday, I want to be able to look back and recall what I did. Reflect upon learnings, missteps and wins. At least, this is how I will do it. Write for myself on the world wide web.

To be immortal. I want that. To never be forgotten. And, only through creation you can achieve it. Would be funny that my kids could read all of this. 

For years I wanted to build my digital real estate, but for one reason or another I always postponed - lack of time, lack of tech skills, no code tools are too complex or lack the flexibility.

But the truth, I should just have started something. Anything. Should have taken the path of least resistance and build slowly brick by brick.

The best time was yesterday. Now, is the second best time to start. 

I have all the tech available to power me in this journey and I'm taking full advantage of it. My writing setup might be an overkill - but more about that someday later.

**Build.Fun.Free**
