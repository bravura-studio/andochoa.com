# Hello World

Finally! Welcome. 

I will take you with me on my product builder journey. Just writing my diary / memoirs. As if I wanted to look back and recall what I did. Reflect upon learnings, missteps and wins. At least, this is how I will do it. Write for myself on the world wide web.

Immortal. I want that. To never be forgotten. Only through creation you achieve it. Would be funny someday my kids reading all of this. 

For years I wanted to build my digital real estate, but for one reason or another I always postponed - lack of time, lack of tech skills, no code tools are too complex or lack the flexibility.

But the truth, I should just have started something. Anything. Should have taken the path of least resistance and build slowly brick by brick.

Now, I feel it is the right time. All the tech available is powering me in this journey and taking full advantage of it. Again, my writing setup might be an overkill - more about the stack later. But, now I am also using it as a testing bed to learn new skills.

**Build.Fun.Free**
